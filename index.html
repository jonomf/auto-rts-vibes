How to play

The idea

  • You don't command units. You write the program that does, then watch it run. Open it with P.
  • Each unit type follows the first rule whose conditions hold, top to bottom. A higher rule interrupts a lower one.
  • Buildings train from their own rules whenever their queue is empty. The Base tab holds the build order, base-wide rules and the layout.
  • Goal: destroy every target on the right. The clock is your score. Restart reruns the match with your current program.

Economy

  • There is no global bank. Every building keeps its own stockpile, and trains only from it.
  • Blueprints are built by delivery: a gatherer places it, then resources hauled to it pay for it as it grows.
  • Haul carries a crate (50 minerals or 25 gas) from one building's stockpile to another that needs it.
  • Tech: Infantry Training → Mech Factory (needs gas) → Port.

Camera

  • Trackpad: two-finger swipe to pan, pinch to zoom. Or hold the right button and drag.
  • Mouse: wheel to zoom; drag with the right or middle button to pan.
  • Anything: Alt + drag, or the arrow keys, to pan.
