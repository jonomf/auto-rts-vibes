How the economy works

  • There is no global bank. Every building keeps its own stockpile, and trains only from it.
  • Gatherers deliver to the nearest Home by default. Right-click another building with them to feed it instead.
  • Blueprints are built by delivery: the builder places it, then gatherers haul its cost to it. It grows as it fills.
  • Tech: Infantry Training → Mech Factory (needs gas) → Port.
